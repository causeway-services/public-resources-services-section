# Public Consumer Resources — actual goods and product designs

This is the **Causeway Public Services consumer/product instance** for goods intended to be useful to people. It is not an alternative source for reusable Engineering code, nor does a product sketch grant publication or a sale. It owns the *specific* product/customer briefs and their corresponding design/output custody, rights and receiving stage.

**Live sourced families:**
- [Fine Print Mercantile print-on-demand](services/public-print-on-demand-services-section/README.md): its original **36 typographic designs and 12 illustrated raster assets** remain in its own catalog and product artifact directory; Red Cup owns the [extracted reusable print-design engine](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/print-design-engineering-services-section) and Public Media holds the [independent Printify draft adapter](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-media-services-section/blob/main/src/pod-printify.mjs). These are not a proven live sold SKU. [Heart-A proposal](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-media-services-section/issues/16) is a separate authored merch proposal requiring its own product supplier/receiver.
- [Transit Layer non-knit apparel construction sample](content/designs/apparel/transit-layer-v0-1/CONSTRUCTION.md): **real 1:1 SVG and DXF pattern pieces** for an ungraded **woven** vest with a physical maker/fit trial defined but not performed. Its geometry is instance design; a reusable CAD compiler belongs to Red Cup. It is not a graphics-on-blanks product, STL, or knitting chart.
- [Knitting Science CITY LOOPS](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/issues/117) is an upstream, separately owned technical research/product proposal, not this instance's accepted retail SKU.

**Shared signal interpretation, separate maker types:** external observations and product demand are first qualified through [561 Opportunity Services](https://github.com/561-group/561-group-opportunity-services-section); garment form/comfort/material hypotheses through [Fibercraft Science](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/main/services/engineering-science-services-section/services/fibercraft-services-section/README.md). Sewn apparel output is pattern-piece geometry, seams, grading, fabric BOM and actual fit. Graphic POD output is original vector/PNG, accepted blank, print method/area, variants, cost and provider draft. Material 3D-print design uses parametric printable solids and slicer/machine physical acceptance under [Group fabrication #2](https://github.com/561-group/561-group-services-section/issues/2). **Do not convert these three material semantics to one interchangeable image, STL, or generic AI prompt.**

**Receiving:** consumer-approved design + independent maker/sample + real material/variant costs + real rights + signed production/readback precede listing. The existing [shop.561.group multi-seller Work #17](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-services-section/issues/17) owns the future physical-products commerce extension; its current catalog is book-specific, so a design in this directory is **not automatically a purchasable offering**. Keep exact source/product revisions at these paths and link original Work; do not create a shadow trend/product ledger, upload graphics as apparel CAD, or infer demand from style popularity.
